F151 / L141: NATIVE DIFFERENTIAL-FORM PULLBACK JETS
Status: implemented candidate; executed acceptance is recorded in docs/VALIDATION.md.

record a=formpullback(native_form,native_map,domain_point,degree[,options]);

native_map takes all n real domain coordinates and explicit trailing parameters,
and returns m real target coordinates. Bounds:1<=n<=m<=6. The map must be a
numerically resolved immersion. A pullback as an exterior-algebra operation also
exists for singular/general maps, but this API deliberately retains a resolved
induced positive-definite metric for subsequent Hodge/codifferential operations;
singular maps reject explicitly. No automatic atlas path is inferred.

native_form takes all m target coordinates and parameters, and returns one real
coefficient per increasing lexicographic degree-p target index tuple. Its value
and first/second analytic jets are evaluated at native_map(domain_point). A
native real tuple or fixed-shape real matrix may supply the complete coefficients.
Degree is0..m; if p>n the pulled-back form is the known zero form of that degree.

The pullback multiplies each composed coefficient by the corresponding Jacobian
minor, with exterior signs preserved. Both first and second coefficient jets
are retained. Native map AD1/AD2 plus cached analytic trilinear AD3 supply the
third map derivatives required to differentiate Jacobian minors twice. No finite
differences or zero-filled unknown higher derivatives are substituted. Symmetric
third entries are evaluated once per increasing triple and expanded explicitly.

options(parameters=record,map_parameters=record,metric=matrix_or_native_metric,
metric_parameters=record,orientation=1,max_native_operations=execution_allowance,
tolerance=1e-12). The target metric defaults to Euclidean identity. A native metric
takes m target coordinates and parameters and returns the complete m-by-m real
metric with compiler-generated AD2. The induced metric J^T(g o map)J and both of
its derivative orders are retained. Metric/Jacobian conditioning,symmetry,finite
arithmetic,work/cancellation and output capacities are checked. Orientation is
explicit +/-1 on the domain; it is not guessed from a nonsquare map determinant.

Output is PentarnionManifoldForm-v2, compatible with wedge,exteriord,hodge,
codifferential,formlaplacian and data-only workspace persistence. point is the
domain point. cache_key retains the field/map/AD3 structural identities;
metric_cache_key also retains target-metric identity. Parameters and coordinates
are runtime data and do not recompile unchanged callback bodies. source reports
native_analytic_pullback_jets. The result is local pointwise differential data,
not a global cohomology or atlas consistency certification.

Goldens: y=x^3,y^2 dy pulls back to3*x^8 dx, including its two derivatives;
-y dx+x dy under the polar map becomes r^2 dtheta; a curved R2-to-R3 immersion
retains its nonconstant induced metric Hessian. Rejection covers singular maps,
incomplete coefficient jets,inconsistent symmetric tensors,work and cancellation.
