F127 / L117: DIFFERENTIAL FORMS WITH VARIABLE METRIC JETS

formfield(native_coefficients,point,degree,options(metric=native_metric,
metric_parameters=options(...),parameters=options(...))) supports Riemannian
metric fields on intrinsic coordinate charts of dimension1..6. Coefficients
follow lexicographically ordered increasing index tuples. Callbacks return
real scalars or explicitly shaped real coefficient matrices; the compiler
emits first/second field and metric derivatives in cached native DLLs/SOs.

The resulting PentarnionManifoldForm-v2 record appends metric_first,
metric_second,metric_cache_key to all original form data. The established
PentarnionRealForm-v1 constant-metric record and ABI remain valid unchanged.
v2 records retain the coordinate point, full metric jets, orientation and
native callback identities through operations and workspace persistence.

hodge(form) differentiates both coefficients and the metric's volume/inverse
factors. A sampled metric is not silently frozen when it came from a native
field. exteriord(form) consumes one known derivative order; wedge(a,b) uses
the graded antisymmetric product and product-rule jets. Wedge operands must
agree on sampled metric/orientation and any retained variable metric jets.
Empty forms above the chart dimension are retained as known graded zeros.

codifferential(form) implements delta=(-1)^(n*(p+1)+1) star d star for p-forms.
It requires degree>=1 and known first jets, and consumes one derivative order.
formlaplacian(form) implements the Hodge operator d delta+delta d using known
second jets. It returns values with derivative_order=0, since third/fourth jets
were not supplied. Unknown higher derivatives are never silently set to zero.
On scalar functions this nonnegative geometric operator has the opposite sign
to div(grad f). Example:in polar Euclidean coordinates,g=diag(1,r^2),
star(dr)=r*dtheta,delta(dr)=-1/r,and formlaplacian(r^2)=-4.

Variable metric jets are propagated through native Cholesky/inverse/minor
calculus; symmetry, positive definiteness, conditioning and finite arithmetic
are checked. All work/cancellation and coefficient capacities are bounded.
Existing constant-metric forms still support their prior dimension bounds;
new variable-metric/codifferential/Laplacian kernels are bounded to1..6.
General pseudo-Riemannian signatures and automatic global cohomology are not
implied by this local pointwise differential calculus.
