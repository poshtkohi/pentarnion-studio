F155 / L145: NATIVE TRIANGLE-MESH INTERSECTIONS
Status: implemented candidate; numerical acceptance is recorded in docs/VALIDATION.md.

record result=meshintersect(first_mesh,second_mesh[,options]);

Inputs are explicit real three-dimensional triangle embeddings, with positive
one-based face indices and resolved nondegenerate triangles. This is physical
surface geometry. A five-coefficient algebra value is not a spatial solid by
virtue of its coefficient count. BVH candidate filtering and independent triangle
pair predicates retain noncoplanar segments/tangent points and coplanar overlap
polygons/shared edges/points. Coplanar area polygons have at most six vertices.

Predicates translate and scale each candidate pair, use long-double arithmetic,
unit plane normals and an explicit spatial tolerance band (default1e-9), with a
roundoff floor. They are numerical tolerance predicates, not certified exact
arithmetic. Near-coplanar inputs inside that band are treated as coplanar. Very
thin/unresolved triangles reject explicitly. Points are deterministic within
each ordered first-face/second-face pair, independently of worker scheduling.
The operation does not merge face-pair contacts into watertight union topology.
It is not a Boolean CSG/remeshing/self-intersection-certification API.

options(first_values=nodal_field,second_values=nodal_field,max_primitives=10000,
tolerance=1e-9,threads=shared_setting,max_work=execution_allowance,
max_bytes=512MiB,max_seconds=60). Nodal fields have one complete element per
vertex or real vertices-by-components data. All1..64 coordinates and immutable
algebra hash/basis metadata are retained independently for the two surfaces.
Interpolation uses each original face's barycentric system; no coefficient is
dropped and no algebra norm/multiplication is inferred. Inside tolerance bands,
small barycentric extrapolation can occur and is not disguised as exact contact.

Result points has three real coordinate columns. first_barycentric and
second_barycentric have three columns each. first_values/second_values retain
all corresponding coefficient columns. offsets/counts locate each primitive's
contiguous points using one-based offsets; first_faces/second_faces identify its
original triangle pair. dimensions=0,1,2 means point,segment,area polygon;
coplanar flags the plane-band classification. primitive_count,point_count,
coplanar_pairs,native_work,threads_used,parallel_calls and explicit semantics
are retained. Per-surface algebra_id/hash/basis_labels remain separate.

Bounds:100000 vertices and200000 triangles per surface,50000 primitives,
300000 retained contact vertices,explicit conservative BVH/worker/output memory,
native work/wall-time/cancellation limits and Studio's lower array allowances.
Candidate pairs use the shared CPU pool; no private pool or BLAS thread mutation.
Output-bound exhaustion fails explicitly rather than truncating intersections.
Closed-solid inside/outside/boundary queries remain meshcontains with its explicit
oriented-manifold validation and winding semantics.

Acceptance:tests/runtime/mesh_intersection_kernel_tests.cpp and
mesh_intersection_language_tests.cpp. Example:
