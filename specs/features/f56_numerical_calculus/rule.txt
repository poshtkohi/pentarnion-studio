F56: Sample interpolation, quadrature and spatial calculus
Authorized extension, 26 October 2026. Language 0.2.

Provide interp1 linear interpolation/extrapolation with strict monotone finite
coordinates; deval cubic Hermite interpolation uses retained sample values and
RHS endpoint slopes. It is not the solver's internal dense polynomial; report
sample-spacing dependence and reject missing legacy slopes/outside queries.
Provide bounded adaptive Simpson integral for real/Pentarnion callbacks;
callbacks stay synchronous, errors/cancellation/convergence exhaustion diagnose.
Add trapz/cumtrapz, diff, gradient, divergence and unscaled laplacian, with explicit
1/2 axes, shapes, monotone nonuniform coordinates, signed spacings and one-sided
three-point boundary stencils. Document empty/singleton/two-point behavior.
Linear Pentarnion calculus acts on five coefficients without changing products.
Heavy kernels reside in lazy penta_numerics; share the host thread/cancel budget.

Authority: language.txt L43 and specs/foundations_v0_2.txt section 7.
Acceptance: foundation_numerics_tests, calculus_kernel_tests and numerical-calculus example;
analytic polynomial checks, Hermite refinement, malformed buffers, callback
failure and cancellation. Out of scope: PDE solves, objective gradients, internal
solver dense output and rigorous quadrature error bounds. Status: docs/VALIDATION.md.

SECOND-DERIVATIVE INSUFFICIENT-DATA CORRECTION — 26 OCTOBER 2026
For nonempty PENTA_CALC_DERIVATIVE2 requests, the differentiated axis must
contain at least three samples. Two samples determine a secant, not curvature.
The unchanged C ABI returns PENTA_NUMERIC_ARGUMENT with an Insufficient data
diagnostic before writing any output; laplacian propagates the runtime error.
Real/Pentarnion coefficients and row/column axes follow the same rule. Empty
arrays stay empty; the two-sample first derivative remains the secant. diff(A,2)
is a different operation and retains its ordinary empty-difference semantics.
Regression coverage: calculus_kernel_tests (buffer sentinels/axes/widths),
foundation_numerics_tests (language errors and valid first/quadratic derivatives).
