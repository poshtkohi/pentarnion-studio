F62: Explicit Pentarnion left/right matrix operators
Authorized extension, 26 October 2026.

leftop(A) maps column x coefficients to A*x and has size5m-by5n for m-by-n A.
rightop(A) maps row x coefficients to x*A and has size5n-by5m. Entry coordinates
are(1,i,j,k,ell), using established scalar multiplication maps. solveleft(A,B)
solves A*X=B and solveright(A,B) solves X*A=B with explicit RHS packing and real
solver safeguards. Direct Pentarnion array det/inv/decompositions are rejected.
det(leftop(A)),det(rightop(A)),inv(leftop(A)),inv(rightop(A)) are real coordinate
operator quantities; do not label them associative whole-Pentarnion inverses or
determinants. Preserve grouping and do not infer inverse reversal, determinant
product identities or leftop(A*B)=leftop(A)*leftop(B). Scalar inv remains unchanged.
