FEATURE F08: Finite range syntax and types
Version: 0.1
Status: implemented in interpreter0.2; actual verification in docs/VALIDATION.md
Implementation stage: S3
Dependencies: F07
Authority: language.txt; specs/algebras/A_gamma_v1.txt where applicable.

PURPOSE
Record finite search/iteration domains syntactically without implementing
search, loops or runtime enumeration in this release.

REQUIRED BEHAVIOUR
F08-R1: integer range name=start:step:stop and real range declarations follow L12.
F08-R2: Integer endpoints/step required for integer ranges. Scalar widening is
         allowed in real range descriptor components, not range-type conversion.
F08-R3: Exactly three parts in the colon form. Range-copy initialization and
         same-type range reassignment are allowed; scalar/range assignment is not.
F08-R4: Literal signed zero step is diagnosed. Unknown step values remain a
         future runtime check; do not execute variables/functions to decide them.
F08-R5: No materialisation of huge ranges or numerical iteration in checker.
F08-R6: Help distinguishes finite sequence descriptors from continuous intervals.

ACCEPTANCE
F08-T1: orders=1:1:5, descending 5:-1:1 and strengths=.1:.1:1 type-check.
F08-T2: integer range xs=0:.5:1; and real b=0:.1:1; fail.
F08-T3: Huge range bounds do not allocate a huge array or hang the editor.
F08-T4: zero-step fixture gets E1404; no claims that variable step is safe.

CHANGE CONTROL
This feature implements the normative contracts, not an alternative language.
Update this rule and its tests when behaviour changes. Record any unresolved
source discrepancy. Completion requires the acceptance evidence, not stubs.
