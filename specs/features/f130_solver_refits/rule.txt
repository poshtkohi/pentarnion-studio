F130: Native parallel ODE/PDE fitting and refit ensembles
Authorized inventory-group1/8 extension,27 October2026.
Status: implemented bounded native adapter; platform acceptance is recorded in docs/VALIDATION.md.

ODE/PDE multistart, profile and bootstrap commands share the native Ceres/inference
scheduler, cached scientific callbacks, per-point trajectory caches, shared CPU
budget, owner-thread iteration observers and completed-task checkpoint ledgers.
ODE Jacobians use compiler AD/CVODES sensitivities; PDE Jacobians are explicitly
finite differences of complete native solves. Full coordinate layouts are retained.
Profiles/rank diagnostics and fitted-mean bands do not prove identifiability or
future-observation coverage. Bounds/unsupported model contracts are explicit.
