# Research papers

The following three arXiv preprints by Alireza Poshtkohi and Susanne Pumplün
describe the Pentarnion algebra and related geometry and differential equations.
When using the corresponding mathematics, cite the relevant papers alongside
the exact Pentarnion Studio version or source commit used in the calculation.

1. Alireza Poshtkohi and Susanne Pumplün (2026).
   **A Five-Dimensional Nonassociative Algebra: elliptic integrability and
   bracketing-dependent dynamics.** arXiv:2610.34202. Submitted 25 October 2026.
   [Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34202).
2. Alireza Poshtkohi and Susanne Pumplün (2026).
   **From Quaternions to Pentarnions I: Algebra, geometry, and a spin model.**
   arXiv:2610.31485. Submitted 22 October 2026.
   [Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31485).
3. Alireza Poshtkohi and Susanne Pumplün (2026).
   **From Quaternions to Pentarnions II: quadratic differential equations.**
   arXiv:2610.34201. Submitted 25 October 2026.
   [Read the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34201).
